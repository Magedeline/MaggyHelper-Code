## Why This Album Fits

The mod is **named after the artist**. The album's arc — ambient introspection building into
cosmic-scale confrontation — mirrors the mod's storyline of Kirby and Madeline ascending beyond
the mountain of the void. Multiple cutscene files directly reference album track names.

Fan summary:
> *"The first half: lofi beats to study/relax to. The second half: the f\*\*\*ing end of the world."*

Album outro:
> *"One day, in an open field, I will find the black and white flower that blooms only during a
> solar eclipse. And connect with my visions, just to see myself in my own dream. Only then
> will I finally step Into The Light to discover my true self. The Divine Light."*

## Audio Implementation Notes

### Existing FMOD Event Paths
```
event:/desolozantas/music/CSide/
event:/desolozantas/music/DXSide/
event:/desolozantas/music/lvl8/chara_acoustic
event:/music/lvl9/main
```

### Layer System
The mod uses dynamic music layering (`musicLayer1` through `musicLayer4`).
Systematica Patterns tracks with building intensity (e.g., Doppia Elica, AntiNihility)
are ideal candidates for multi-layer implementation where layers add as boss phases progress.

### Integration Approach
- **Ambient first-half tracks** → exploration/hub rooms, `music` field
- **Intense second-half tracks** → boss arenas and climactic cutscenes, `alt_music` field
- **Transitions** → Use `MusicLayerTrigger` for gradual builds
- **`InfernoEndingMusicHandler`** → could tie to Destino Divino for the fated ending

> *"The Broken Star Warrior"* as a track name is essentially **Kirby's theme for this mod** —
> a star warrior, broken by the journey, still pushing forward. This parallels Madeline's
> Celeste arc perfectly. In Ch19 beat 7, Kirby literally *becomes* the Broken Star Warrior
> when he realizes Madeline and Badeline are truly gone forever.
